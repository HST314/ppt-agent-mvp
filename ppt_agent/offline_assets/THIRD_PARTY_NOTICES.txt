The bundled assets/motion.min.js is the UMD build of motion@11.11.17
(dist/motion.js, exposes window.Motion) so it can load as a classic script
over file:// without module CORS restrictions. It replaces the former ES
module build copied from the supplied PPT Skill local assets. The supplied
